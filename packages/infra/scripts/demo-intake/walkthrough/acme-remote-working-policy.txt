Acme Corp - Remote Working Policy
Version 1.0, effective 1 October 2026
Owner: Compliance Manager

1. Purpose
This policy sets out how Acme staff work safely away from the office.

2. Rules
2.1 Remote access to Acme systems uses the company VPN with multi-factor sign-in.
2.2 Work is done on company laptops only; personal devices may reach email through the web only.
2.3 Screens are locked whenever the laptop is left, even at home.
2.4 Public Wi-Fi is used only with the VPN connected.

3. Equipment
3.1 Company laptops stay encrypted and run endpoint detection and response.
3.2 Lost or stolen equipment is reported to the service desk within one hour.

4. Review
This policy is reviewed every year by the Compliance Manager.
