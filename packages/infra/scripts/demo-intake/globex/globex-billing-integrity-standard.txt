Globex Ltd - Billing Integrity Standard
Version 1.0, effective 1 May 2026
Owner: Admin
Classification: Restricted

1. Purpose
This standard sets out how Globex keeps billing data correct and complete, from the delivery scan
to the customer's invoice.

2. The risk
2.1 Billing data tampering is the risk this standard treats.
2.2 The Billing database and the Billing service are exposed to Billing data tampering.

3. Controls
3.1 Billing data tampering is mitigated by Daily billing reconciliation and by Database activity
    monitoring.
3.2 Daily billing reconciliation matches every invoice line to a delivery scan before the billing
    run.

3.3 Database activity monitoring alerts the on-call engineer when a billing record changes outside
    the billing service.

5. Review
