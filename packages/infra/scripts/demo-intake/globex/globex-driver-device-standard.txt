Globex Ltd - Driver Device Standard
Version 1.4, effective 1 April 2026
Owner: Compliance Manager

1. Purpose
This standard covers the handheld scanners and phones Globex drivers carry, and the data on them.
It supports the Mobile Device Policy.

2. Enrolment
2.1 Every scanner is enrolled in mobile device management before it leaves the depot.
2.2 A scanner that is not enrolled cannot sign in to the warehouse management system.

3. Use
3.1 Scanners are used only for deliveries, collections and proof of delivery.
3.2 Drivers sign in with their own account at the start of every shift.

4. Loss and theft
4.1 A lost or stolen scanner is reported to the depot supervisor straight away.
4.2 The device team locks and wipes it remotely within one hour of the report.

5. Data
Delivery photos and signatures are uploaded at the end of each stop and are not kept on the device.
