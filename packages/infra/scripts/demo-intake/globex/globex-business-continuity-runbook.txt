Globex Ltd - Business Continuity Runbook
Version 2.0, effective 1 February 2026
Owner: Compliance Manager

1. Purpose
This runbook describes how Globex keeps deliveries moving when the warehouse management system or
a depot is unavailable.

2. Triggers
2.1 The warehouse management system is unavailable for more than 30 minutes.
2.2 A depot loses power or network for more than one hour.

3. Steps
3.1 The duty manager declares a continuity event and opens the event record.
3.2 Operations switch to the warehouse failover site.
3.3 Drivers keep scanning; scans queue on the device and upload when the system returns.
3.4 Customers see a status note on the tracking page.

4. Recovery
4.1 Switch back to the main site once it has been stable for two hours.
4.2 Reconcile queued scans with the billing service before the next billing run.

5. Review
Every continuity event is reviewed within 10 working days.
