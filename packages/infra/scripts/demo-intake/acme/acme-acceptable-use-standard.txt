Acme Corp - Acceptable Use Standard
Version 2.1, effective 1 March 2026
Owner: Compliance Manager

1. Purpose
This standard sets out how Acme staff and contractors may use company laptops, accounts,
networks and data. It supports the Information Security Policy.

2. Scope
It applies to everyone with an Acme account, on any device that connects to Acme systems.

3. Accounts and sign-in
3.1 Every account belongs to one named person. Accounts are never shared.
3.2 Remote access to Acme systems requires multi-factor sign-in.
3.3 Passwords are at least 12 characters and are never reused on other sites.

4. Devices
4.1 Company laptops run endpoint detection and response at all times.
4.2 Security updates are installed within the monthly patching window.
4.3 A lost or stolen device is reported to the service desk within one hour.

5. Data
5.1 Confidential and restricted data stays on Acme systems and approved cloud storage.
5.2 Customer data is never copied to personal devices or personal email.

6. Monitoring
Acme monitors the use of its systems to protect them. Monitoring follows applicable law.

7. Breaches of this standard
A breach of this standard may lead to disciplinary action.
