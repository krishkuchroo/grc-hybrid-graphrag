Acme Corp - Payroll Access Standard
Version 1.2, effective 1 June 2026
Owner: Admin
Classification: Restricted

1. Purpose
This standard sets out who may open payroll data at Acme and how that access is checked. It
supports the Access Control Policy.

2. The risk
2.1 Payroll fraud by an insider is the risk this standard treats.
2.2 The Payroll database is exposed to Payroll fraud by an insider.

3. Controls
3.1 Payroll fraud by an insider is mitigated by Segregation of duties in payroll and by Quarterly
    privileged access reviews.
3.2 Segregation of duties in payroll: no one person can both add a payee and approve a payment run.
3.3 Quarterly privileged access reviews: every quarter, the payroll manager and the Admin confirm
    each account that can open the Payroll database, and remove the ones no longer needed.
3.4 Both controls are governed by the Access Control Policy.
